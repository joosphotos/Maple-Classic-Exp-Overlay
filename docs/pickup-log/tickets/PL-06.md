# PL-06 Finding the log

**Blocked by:** PL-04 (font geometry), PL-02b (Windows, 2x, small window and controller recordings with truth).
**Blocks:** PL-09. **Size:** M.

## Do
`pickup_find.py` (pure): `find_log(img, origin, window_size, scale_hint=None, last=None) -> Anchor or None`
(`Anchor` in `pickup_types.py`; `img` a picture of part of the game window whose top-left is at `origin` in window
pixels).
1. Measure where the log sits in every truth recording (Mac/Windows, Default/Ideal 1x/2x, small window, controller
   bar) and find the rule: right end vs the window's right edge, the bottom vs the window's bottom or the
   quick-slot bar, as functions of the UI scale (`ui_layout`). Note the Mac window includes its title bar
   (`platforms/macos.py`).
2. Expected place from the rule, then confirmed by the lines themselves: right-aligned shadowed rows on the pitch
   (the shadow feature, see PLAN.md). With `last`, check there first (cheap). No lines on screen: None (nothing to
   confirm yet); a wrong anchor is worse than none.
3. **From PL-02:** Mac anchors are known (Ideal right 1914, bottom 958, pitch 14; Default right 1911, bottom 897,
   pitch 19.69; the Mac window has a 28 px title bar). The log moves when the game window is dragged (Default looks
   247-253: 73 px left for a moment): find it again rather than trust `last`. Name tags drawn over the log look like
   right-aligned lines but not on the log's right edge and pitch.
4. `log_box(window_size, anchor_or_expected)`: the small rectangle to grab each look (the stack's full height at the
   largest limit seen, plus a margin).

## Done when
- `tests/test_pickup_find.py`: every truth recording's looks with lines give the truth anchor (exact at 1x and 2x,
  within half a pixel when smoothed); looks without lines give None; pictures without the log (other parts of the
  frames, synthetic scenes) never give an anchor; made-up window sizes give the rule's expected place.
- Cost in Results: the check at `last` (target under 1 ms) and the full search (target under 20 ms).
