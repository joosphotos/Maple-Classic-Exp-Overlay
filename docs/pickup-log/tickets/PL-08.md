# PL-08 Following the stack

**Blocked by:** PL-05 (synthetic logs for the stress tests). Uses PL-02's truth (anchors by hand until PL-06 is in).
**Blocks:** PL-09. **Size:** L (the hardest part: take the time to get it exactly right).

## Why
This is what makes every line count exactly once: from one look to the next, how many new lines came in, or
"can't tell" (a gap). It runs at every look, so it must be cheap.

## Do
`pickup_stack.py` (pure): `StackTracker(anchor, scale)` with `.look(img, t) -> StackLook` (`pickup_types.py`) and
which rows are new; `.reset()` after a gap in looking (paused, window lost).
1. **Per line, a fade-proof signature:** the shadow feature (pixel minus its shadow pixel, PLAN.md) of the line's
   band, so a line matches itself while fading and over a moving scene. Its strength = how strong that pattern is.
2. **The shift:** the s in 0..(lines shown) for which this look's lines k match the last look's lines k + s, with
   the age checks that make runs of identical lines unambiguous:
   - the s newest lines are at new strength (fitted per colour and scale from PL-02/PL-05),
   - every older line's strength fell by what the time between the looks says (the fade from `pickup_types.py`,
     fitted again),
   - a stack that only got shorter from the top, with no new strength at the bottom, is s = 0 (lines faded out).
3. **Gaps (lines may be lost):** the best s equals the lines shown and nothing lines up; or the time since the last
   look is longer than the shortest a line could have lived with that many arrivals; or scores too close to choose.
   A gap says why. Never a confident wrong count: when unsure, say so (gap) rather than pick.
4. **Covered lines:** a line partly hidden (sprite, damage number, a window) still matches on what is visible; a
   line hidden entirely for a look is carried by position and age.
5. **From PL-02** (Results and CATALOGUE.md): the game shows at most **6** lines (a 7th drops the oldest at once);
   a new line's first-look strength was never below 239 (Ideal) / 208 (Default on the Ideal scale); the fade varies
   12-17 a look, so use strength for "new or not", not exact age; the rough proposer failed on **identical neighbours in
   a bright scene** (Default looks 258, 266, 271) and on pairs counted as one: use stack height and shape too; name tags
   drawn over the log look like lines; the log can move with the window for a few looks (Default 247-253): that is a
   gap and a reset, never a count.
6. Report the lines shown at each look (the live part learns the game's limit from it).

## Done when
- `tests/test_pickup_stack.py`, synthetic (always): at 1x and 3x, looks at 10 a second and with the 10/20 policy,
  with jitter and hiccups: **every seeable line counted exactly once** (100%); every unseeable one inside a reported
  gap; no gap where nothing was lost, except after a real hiccup; runs of 3+ identical lines; bursts up to 14. At
  5x: the same numbers reported in Results (not required to pass).
- On the recordings (skips without `MAPLE_CAPTURES`): the arrivals equal the truth's at every look.
- Cost in Results: target under 0.5 ms a look at 1x, median and 99th percentile.
