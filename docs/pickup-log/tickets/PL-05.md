# PL-05 Synthetic pickup logs

**Blocked by:** PL-04 (the font, with fading). **Blocks:** PL-08, PL-09. **Size:** M.

## Why
No character today can mob, but the reader must hold at 3x the measured rate and say when it can't. Synthetic logs
drawn with the real font and fade, on real-looking scenes, with every line known, test exactly that, and run on CI.

## Do
`pickup_synth.py` (pure) and `tests/test_pickup_synth.py`:
1. **The log model** (numbers from `pickup_types.py`, fitted again from PL-02's truth: say the fit in Results):
   newest line at the bottom; a new line pushes the stack up one pitch at once; each line fades from its arrival
   (strength curve fitted from the recordings) and goes at strength 0 or when the stack holds more than its
   limit (`limit` a parameter: **6**, as the game does (PL-02): a 7th line drops the oldest at once, even while it is
   still fading; also try 5 and 8); fills from the font's colours, with the rules in CATALOGUE.md (party bonus yellow,
   mesos and items white, EXP either); the fade per look varies 12-17 between lines (PL-02), not one steady rate.
2. **Arrivals:** kills at a rate (each kill = EXP + party bonus at the same instant, sometimes + mesos or an item
   later); the measured rate 2.7 lines a second x 1, x 3, x 5; multi-kill bursts of 2-7 kills at once (4-14
   lines); runs of identical lines; amounts and item names drawn from realistic ranges (`assets/items.txt` if PL-03
   is in, else a few made-up names).
3. **Scenes:** include character name tags drawn **over** the log (white text on a dark box: they look like lines,
   CATALOGUE.md) and bright scenery in the upper slots. Made-up (noise, gradients, stripes, moving blocks: for CI) and real (pieces of the recordings away
   from the log, panned like a moving camera: only with `MAPLE_CAPTURES`). Things over the log: sprite-sized blobs
   crossing it, damage-number-like digits, a window covering it for a while.
4. **Looks:** the camera's schedule: 10 a second with jitter, hiccups (200-400 ms, rare), and 20 a second for 2 s
   after a look with 3+ new lines (the PLAN's policy), plus any schedule the caller passes.
5. **Out:** a stream of (t, picture of the log box, truth) where truth lists the lines on screen at that moment and,
   per line, whether it is *seeable* (shown uncovered at some look) or not (pushed out or covered before any look
   saw it). Deterministic by seed. Scales 1x, 1080/768 (Default 1080) and 2x.
6. **Realism check:** compare synthetic and real strength curves and the shadow feature's statistics on the same
   scales (numbers in Results); a debug option writes a few PNGs to look at (not committed).

## Done when
The generator, its tests (deterministic, fast: a 30 s stream in a few seconds), the realism numbers in Results.
