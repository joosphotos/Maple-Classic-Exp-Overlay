# PL-02 Truth and message catalogue (Mac recordings)

**Blocked by:** nothing. **Blocks:** PL-04, PL-07, PL-08 (and PL-02b repeats it for Windows). **Size:** M.

## Why
Every later ticket is judged against the truth: which lines arrived at which look, and what they say. Without it
"100%" can't be measured. The catalogue lists every kind of line, so the reader's grammar is complete.

## Inputs
Private repo `joosphotos/maple-captures` (attach with **push** access; clone; `MAPLE_CAPTURES` = its folder):
`captures/log-20261029-130655` (Default UI) and `captures/log-20261029-131006` (Ideal UI), each 299 looks at 10 a
second of the game's bottom right (`frames.json` says where each frame sits in the window), plus `game_start.png`
and `game_end.png` (whole window: the EXP bar's value at start and end). `log-20261029-130809` is empty (ignore).
`tools/pickup_probe.py` measures the rough numbers (see its docstring for each recording's parameters).

## Do
1. `tools/pickup_label.py` (repo tool, pure numpy/OpenCV):
   - proposes arrivals look by look (start from `pickup_probe.arrivals`' shift matching, made as good as is cheap),
   - cuts each proposed new line at its first look, enlarged with nearest-neighbour, into numbered contact sheets
     (PNG) with the look before and after, written **only** under `$MAPLE_CAPTURES/truth/work/`,
   - `validate`: checks a truth file is consistent (see the format) and prints per-look stacks.
2. Write the truth by looking at the sheets (you can view images): every line's text exactly as drawn, its colour,
   and the look it first appears. Fix the proposed arrivals where they are wrong. Mark lines you can't read
   (covered) as `"text": null` with a note. Then validate.
3. Also from these recordings: the EXP bar value in `game_start.png` and `game_end.png`, and the sum of EXP and
   party EXP lines in between (does the log account for all of it? lines before the first look and after the
   last count separately: say how you handled them).
4. `docs/pickup-log/CATALOGUE.md`: every kind of line seen (exact wording, colours, number formats), what white vs
   yellow seems to mean (with the evidence, or "unknown"), lines covered by sprites or damage numbers (drawn over
   the log or under?), the most lines at once, the line pitch and right edge for each recording (in game-window
   pixels: frame position + `frames.json` box - `game` rect), and anything odd. Add to it, never rewrite it, in
   PL-02b.

## Truth format (`$MAPLE_CAPTURES/truth/<recording>.json`)
```json
{"recording": "log-20261029-131006", "ui": "ideal", "scale": 1.0,
 "anchor": {"right": 1912, "bottom": 933, "pitch": 14},
 "exp_bar": {"start": 157677, "end": 0},
 "lines": [{"id": 0, "look": 12, "text": "You received EXP (+5)", "colour": "yellow", "note": ""}]}
```
(The numbers are an example.) Anchor in game-window pixels (Mac: the window includes its title bar; say so). `lines` in arrival order; lines
arriving at the same look are in stack order (top first). Lines already on screen at look 0 get `"look": 0` and
`"before_start": true`. The validator checks the order and that, with each line lasting at most about 2 s,
no look shows more lines than the stack in the frames.

## Done when
- Both recordings have a validated truth file, pushed to `maple-captures` (the work folder too).
- `CATALOGUE.md` and `tools/pickup_label.py` (with a short test in `tests/test_pickup_label.py` on a made-up truth
  file) are in the PR. The validator runs from the test.
- Results: arrivals count per recording, how many lines unreadable and why, the EXP check, and how often the rough
  proposer was wrong (helps PL-08).

- **EXP check, and look 0:** the lines already on screen at look 0 are not in the sums: `game_start.png` already holds their
  EXP. So the log accounts for all EXP, exactly, in both recordings, and `You received EXP` and `Bonus EXP for PARTY` are
  separate EXP that add up to the bar.
- **How it was read:** every line by eye from contact sheets (3x enlarged, with the look before and after), the
  transcription typed into a reads file, colours measured from the pixels (0 disagreements), then checked three ways:
  the stack check in `validate` (counts and order of lines against the strength of every slot of every look), every
  distinct text viewed once more side by side, and the exact EXP sums above. Repeated texts are pixel-identical in Ideal
  (shape overlap 0.96 or more). Mesos amounts and item names rest on the eye and that side-by-side view only.
- **How often the rough proposer was wrong** (final version; 287-288 looks each, after leaving the covered ones out):
  Ideal 1 look wrong (a pair counted as 1), Default 7 looks wrong, all 8 missing lines in a pair or a repeat (look 33,
  98, 147, 256 pairs counted as 1 or 0; 258, 266, 271 a new line identical to the one above). 6 of those 7 looks were
  flagged unsure, 1 was not (258, a bright scene hid the strength jump). It never invented a line once its
  "new lines are strong" limit was in (the first version said 151 and 126 lines for 96 and 88). It was tuned while the truth
  was being made, so these numbers are a little kind. Lessons for PL-08: **identical neighbours and bright scenery are
  where the strength fails**; look at the stack height and the shape too; and a line's strength at its first look was
  never below 239 (Ideal) or 208 (Default, scaled to Ideal).
- **What validate can't prove:** digits and item names (that is the EXP sum and the eye), and a missing line identical to
  its neighbour in a bright scene. The truth lists 7 (Ideal) and 22 (Default) `scenery` look/slot pairs where scenery
  mimics a line: all looked at on the sheets.

**Decisions and things the next tickets must know**
- The game draws **at most 6 lines**; older ones vanish at once when a 7th arrives (CATALOGUE). `pickup_types.MAX_LINES_SEEN = 7`
  became `MAX_LINES = 6` (no other user), `BUSIEST_LOOK` 3 -> 4, `RATE_MEASURED` 2.7 -> 3.0, `FADE_PER_SECOND` comment says 120-170;
  `PLAN.md` says the same. PL-05's synthetic logs must drop the oldest line at 6, and PL-08 can look at 6 slots (this tool uses 8
  only so scenery can show).
- Strength falls 12-17 a look (not a steady 12), so it tells age only to +- 0.2 s; use it for "new or not", not for exact ages.
- Fonts: white is (251, 251, 251), yellow about (254, 253, 90) at full strength; both on a 1 px black shadow. White and yellow
  EXP lines both exist; the colour does **not** follow the amount or the party. Don't use it to tell lines apart or to decide kind.
- Name tags are drawn over the log and look like lines to anything that finds white text with a shadow; damage numbers,
  sprites and effects are under it. A long item name is cut with `...` (`Halloween Can... x1 earned. (Etc)`).
- The Default recording has the game window dragged for 0.6 s (looks 247-253): the box lagged, the log sat 73 px left. PL-06's
  finder needs to cope with a log that moves.
- Anchors (game-window pixels, Mac window with its 28 px title bar): Ideal right 1914, bottom 958, pitch 14; Default right 1911,
  bottom 897, pitch 19.69. In the truth files.
- PL-02b can reuse `build` and the reads format as they are: add the recording's grid to `GRIDS` (bottom, pitch, right,
  scale, start strength; the Mac numbers are a good guess for scale 1 and 1.406), then `propose`, `sheets`, type the reads file,
  `build`, `validate`. The Windows window has no title bar, so the notes text in `build` (Mac title bar) needs its words changed.
